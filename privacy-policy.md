---
title: Privacy policy for Tut by tabngo
---

Effective November 4, 2026

tabngo operates Tut by tabngo, including the iOS app, its App Clip, and the order-tracking website. This policy explains how Tut handles information.

## Order tracking

You can track an order without creating a customer account. When you open an order link, Tut retrieves the café name, order number, and pickup status. Tut stores the order link on your device so it can reopen the order. The café may enter an order reference. Tut records order events such as a ready signal and your pickup acknowledgment so the café and your device can show the current status.

When your device connects to an order, Tut sends and stores a randomly generated app installation identifier and connection timestamps. For Apple notifications and Live Activities, Tut also stores the app or App Clip identifier, the push environment, and available Apple device and Live Activity tokens. Tut associates this information with the order to route updates and ready alerts. This information is used for app functionality. Tut does not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Café dashboard

Café staff sign in to the separate dashboard with ChatGPT. The hosting service supplies account details to the dashboard during sign-in. Tut uses the account identifier to associate staff with their café. Customer order tracking does not require this sign-in.

## Service providers and access

Tut uses hosting and database providers to operate the service and Apple Push Notification service to deliver notifications and Live Activity updates. Café staff can see their own café’s order information, including whether a customer connected or acknowledged pickup. Anyone who has an active order link can view its tracking status. Share your link only with people you trust.

## Retention and choices

Standard order links expire after 12 hours. The service retains order records, status events, and device registrations in its database. Link expiration ends customer access through the link. It does not automatically erase those records.

You can disable notifications or Live Activities in your Apple device settings. To ask about access to or deletion of information, email [moaz@zimetech.com](mailto:moaz@zimetech.com). Include the café name and order number if available. Keep the private order link out of your initial email.

We update this page when the policy changes. For questions, contact [Tut support](support.html).
