## Track an order

1. Open the order link from your cashier or scan the café’s QR code with your iPhone camera.
2. If you have the full app open, you can also paste the order link into Tut and select **Track order**.
3. When your order is ready, select **I’m coming** to tell the café you’re on your way to the counter.

If a link has expired or you need to change your order, contact the café.

## Check alerts

To hear an alert, enable notifications, turn off Silent Mode, and check your volume and Focus settings. Use **Test buzzer sound** in Tut to check your current settings. The Lock Screen and supported Dynamic Island devices can show your order’s Live Activity when Live Activities are enabled.

## Contact tabngo

Email [moaz@zimetech.com](mailto:moaz@zimetech.com) for help with the app. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Keep your private order link out of the email.
